# Project Background
The main goal of this project is to identify the departments and projects that are over budget or underperforming. The dashboard allows us to compare costs and budgets with other departments/projects.

# Executive Summary
## Overview of Findings
The human resources department has lost the company around $58,000 over the past two years, while other departments are in the green. The top department (engineering) has brought in $770,000 in revenue over the past two years despite being third in terms of budget. 64% of our current budget is being allocated to upcoming projects.

# Recommendations
- Because engineering and marketing are the top two departments in terms in revenue, consider allocating a larger budget towards their projects
- Reconsider the budget for the Human Resources department since they are the only unprofitable department
- Consider removing some of the budget from the I.T. department since they are the second worst in terms of revenue, but have the highest budget for their projects
